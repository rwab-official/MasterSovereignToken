## 1. Purpose

This document explains the principles governing the market price, valuation references, price discovery, and public price reporting of Master Sovereign Token (MST).

The purpose is to establish a transparent distinction between:

- Nominal values recorded in smart contract code.
- Project-declared numerical assumptions.
- Indicative price estimates.
- Actual prices resulting from market transactions.
- Independently verified financial asset valuations.

These concepts must not be confused.

## 2. Current Market Price Status

MST does not claim a guaranteed, fixed, or officially established market price.

The deployment of MST on Polygon PoS does not automatically establish:

- An active trading market.
- Exchange listing.
- Sufficient market liquidity.
- A reliable market valuation.
- A guaranteed token price.
- A guaranteed conversion rate against USDT or other currencies.

Any market availability or price information must be supported by actual market data.

## 3. Market Price Determination

Any future market price of MST will be determined by market activity.

Relevant factors may include:

- Supply and demand.
- Actual buy and sell transactions.
- Available market liquidity.
- Trading volume.
- Market depth.
- Exchange availability.
- Overall cryptocurrency market conditions.
- Market participants' expectations and risk assessments.

The project does not control or guarantee the price established by independent market participants.

## 4. No Fixed or Guaranteed Price

MST is not presented as a stablecoin or as a token with a guaranteed minimum price.

The project does not promise:

- A fixed USD value per token.
- A guaranteed EUR value per token.
- A guaranteed USDT conversion rate.
- A minimum trading price.
- A guaranteed return or profit.
- A guaranteed future market capitalization.

Market prices may increase, decrease, or become unavailable.

Token holders may lose some or all of the value they expect to receive when selling their tokens.

## 5. Nominal Contract Values and Market Valuation

Any numerical constants, nominal valuations, or technical parameters contained in the MST smart contract must be distinguished from actual market prices.

In particular, the contract constant:

`NOMINAL_BACKING_VALUATION`

and its source-code expression:

`500000000000 * 10**18`

must not be interpreted as:

- A confirmed market price.
- A verified financial reserve.
- A guaranteed market capitalization.
- A confirmed token valuation.
- A guaranteed redemption amount.
- An independently established asset value.

A numerical value stored in smart contract code does not create market demand or establish an actual trading price.

## 6. Future Price Discovery

If MST becomes available for trading on a decentralized or centralized exchange, its market price may be established through actual trading activity.

Potential price discovery mechanisms include:

### 6.1 Decentralized Exchanges

A decentralized exchange may establish a trading price through liquidity pools and transactions between supported trading pairs.

The displayed price may change according to:

- Pool reserves.
- Transaction size.
- Buy and sell activity.
- Trading fees.
- Price impact.
- Liquidity conditions.

A pool's displayed price does not necessarily represent the price at which a large quantity of MST can be sold.

### 6.2 Centralized Exchanges

If MST is accepted for listing by a centralized exchange, the exchange may display prices based on its order book and completed transactions.

Listing is subject to the independent exchange's requirements and approval.

No exchange listing is promised or guaranteed by this document.

### 6.3 Independent Market Data

Public market-data services may display MST prices if sufficient eligible market data becomes available.

Such services may use different data sources and calculation methods.

Their displayed prices should be evaluated according to the reliability, transparency, and availability of their underlying market data.

## 7. Price Reporting Standards

Any future public MST price announcement should identify:

- Trading pair.
- Exchange or trading platform.
- Market data source.
- Price currency.
- Observation date and time.
- Relevant trading volume.
- Available liquidity, where measurable.
- Whether the figure represents a last transaction, bid, ask, or indicative price.

The project should not publish an indicative estimate as though it were a confirmed executed market price.

## 8. Market Price Versus Reserve Valuation

Market price and reserve valuation are separate concepts.

Market price reflects trading activity and market conditions.

Reserve valuation reflects the assessed value of underlying assets, where such assets exist and have been appropriately documented and verified.

At the date of this disclosure, MST does not claim independently verified reserve backing.

Consequently, no verified reserve-to-token price relationship is established by this document.

Any future reserve valuation must follow the requirements described in:

`BACKING_DISCLOSURE.md`

## 9. Price Oracles and External Data Sources

The project may explore the use of independent market-data services or blockchain price oracles in the future.

Any such integration would require an appropriate technical and security assessment.

Relevant considerations include:

- Data-source reliability.
- Price update frequency.
- Market liquidity.
- Data manipulation risks.
- Oracle availability.
- Network compatibility.
- Smart contract security.
- Independent verification of the reported data.

An oracle-reported price is not automatically a guaranteed redemption value or proof of financial backing.

No operational price oracle is claimed by this document.

## 10. Supply and Market Capitalization

Market capitalization calculations must use a clearly identified token supply figure and a defensible market price.

Any future market capitalization disclosure should identify:

- The price source.
- The applicable trading pair.
- The total supply or circulating supply used.
- The relevant blockchain block height or observation time.
- The calculation methodology.

Fully diluted valuation and circulating market capitalization must not be presented as interchangeable figures.

The existence of a mint function means that token issuance may change. Therefore, supply-related calculations must be updated when relevant.

A nominal contract constant must never be substituted for an actual market price.

## 11. Liquidity and Trading Risks

MST trading, if established, may involve:

- Limited liquidity.
- Significant price volatility.
- Large bid-ask spreads.
- Price slippage.
- Market manipulation.
- Low trading volume.
- Temporary or permanent market inactivity.
- Exchange suspension or removal.

A displayed market price does not guarantee that a token holder can sell at that price.

The project does not guarantee the availability of buyers, sellers, or liquidity providers.

## 12. No Automatic Conversion or Redemption

Holding MST does not automatically establish a right to exchange tokens for:

- US dollars.
- Euros.
- USDT.
- Bank deposits.
- Physical assets.
- Other financial instruments.

Any future conversion or redemption arrangement would require separate operational and legally valid documentation.

The presence of a trading pair, liquidity pool, price display, or oracle integration does not itself establish a redemption obligation.

## 13. Public Price Updates

If reliable market data becomes available, the project may publish market information through its official documentation repository.

Such updates should distinguish:

- Actual executed trading prices.
- Indicative prices.
- Exchange quotations.
- Oracle-reported values.
- Independent asset valuations.

Historical price information should include its observation date and source.

Previous disclosures should remain traceable through the repository's public commit history.
