## 3. Token Supply Structure

MST uses an ERC-20 smart contract that includes an owner-controlled mint function.

The mint function allows the authorized contract owner to create and issue additional MST tokens, subject to the permissions and conditions implemented in the smart contract.

The contract does not establish a fixed maximum token supply through this mint function.

Accordingly:

- The total supply may change through additional authorized mint transactions.
- Token holders should not assume that the supply is permanently fixed.
- Any statement about current total supply must be checked against live blockchain data.
- The circulating supply may differ from the total supply.
- Token balances and transfers do not independently establish market value.

No unverified current supply figure is published in this document.

## 5. Minting and Additional Issuance

The deployed contract includes an owner-restricted mint function.

This function is a material feature of the token's supply model.

Additional issuance may increase the total supply and may affect the relative ownership percentage of existing holders.

The project does not describe MST as a permanently fixed-supply token.

Any future disclosure of significant issuance activity should include, where available:

## 6. Token Burning

The contract includes token-burning functionality.

Burning removes tokens from the relevant token balance and reduces the total supply when successfully executed.

The existence of a burn function does not guarantee that tokens will be burned in the future.

No future burn quantity, schedule, or price effect is promised.
